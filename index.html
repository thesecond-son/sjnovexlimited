About us

Reliable delivery, from pickup to doorstep

SJ Novex Limited is a reliable courier and logistics company committed to providing fast, safe and dependable delivery services across Nigeria and to Accra, Ghana. We specialise in the pickup, transportation and delivery of parcels, documents and business goods, handled with professionalism and care from start to finish.

Beyond courier work, we support businesses and organisations with transportation, general merchandise supply, contracting, and buying-and-selling services; giving clients a single, dependable partner for logistics and supply needs.

Our mission is simple: every package reaches its destination securely and on time, backed by excellent customer service. Your trust is our priority, and every delivery matters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our courier pickup, interstate shipping, and cross-border delivery services.

How fast is courier pickup and delivery in Lagos? +
We arrange rapid doorstep pickup across Lagos (Mainland & Island) once you confirm your details on WhatsApp. Depending on urgency, we offer same-day express rider delivery or scheduled next-day drop-offs.
How does nationwide interstate shipping work? +
We pick up your parcels or commercial goods from your location in Lagos and transport them securely to major cities across Nigeria. Your package is tracked throughout transit, and we confirm delivery once received.
What is the process for Accra, Ghana cross-border delivery? +
We provide direct cross-border courier and cargo services from Lagos to Accra, Ghana. We handle package preparation, documentation, and safe transport to ensure your items arrive intact and on schedule.
How do I get a price quote for my delivery? +
Because rates depend on distance, package dimensions, weight, and urgency, simply tap any "Chat on WhatsApp" button on our site. Our dispatch team will give you an instant, straightforward quote with no hidden add-ons.
Do you offer recurring courier contracts for businesses? +
Yes! We partner with SMEs, corporate organizations, e-commerce stores, churches, and institutions for scheduled daily, weekly, or monthly delivery runs with priority dispatch and dedicated billing accounts.
